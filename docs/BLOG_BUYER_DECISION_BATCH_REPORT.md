## Content approach

- Dutch is the primary and most detailed version, with six content sections and four FAQ items per topic.
- English and Polish are independently localized, with five content sections and at least three FAQ items per topic.
- Pricing is explained as vehicle- and method-dependent; the Power Catalog remains an indication rather than a final quote.
- Fuel-consumption content distinguishes normal use from performance driving and makes no savings guarantee.
- APK and emissions wording avoids pass guarantees and avoids advertising emissions-system removal. It recommends diagnosis, fault repair and solutions allowed for the vehicle and its use.
- Torque-limiter content explains coordinated ECU/TCU calibration without promising suitability for every transmission.
- Turbo, fuel and EGT content emphasizes balance, logging and diagnostics rather than boost pressure alone.

The APK article was reviewed against current official RDW information about inspection scope and environmental requirements. No competitor copy was used.
